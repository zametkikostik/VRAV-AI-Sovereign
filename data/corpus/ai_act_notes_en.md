# EU AI Act notes

CELEX: **32024R1689** (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).

- Unacceptable: social scoring, manipulative subliminal techniques.
- High-risk: biometrics, employment, credit scoring, critical infrastructure.
- Limited risk: chatbots — transparency (inform user they interact with AI).
- Minimal risk: most other systems.

Not legal advice.
